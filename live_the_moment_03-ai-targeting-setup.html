Set up a campaign — in the order the platform asks for it

A big budget with a sloppy setup just burns money. A clean campaign is built in a proven order: pick the objective first, make sure tracking is firing, then define the audience, choose a budget, and build the ad — ending with a live launch you actually watch. Your campaign, first step to launch

💡 Structure, simply: every platform nests the same three levels — campaign (the goal), ad set / ad group (who and how much), and ad (what they see). As a beginner, keep it small: one campaign, one or two ad sets, a couple of ads. AI tools (Advantage+, Performance Max) can build most of this for you — but only if tracking is set up first, because tracking is the fuel the AI learns from.
2

Sort the audience by how warm it is

Not every audience is at the same stage. Someone who has never heard of you is a world apart from someone who added to cart yesterday. Read each audience, then drag it into the right temperature bucket. Matching your message to how warm someone already is toward you is the core skill of targeting. Wrong answers carry a hover hint.

H

Hot audience

Already engaged with you and close to buying. Retargeting / custom audiences: cart abandoners, past buyers, recent site visitors.

  • Visited your page or added to cart
  • Smallest group, but converts best
  • Show them the product and a nudge to finish
W

Warm audience

Knows you a little but hasn't bought. Some prior contact — watched a video, follows the page, on your email list.

  • Engaged with content but no purchase yet
  • Mid-size group, converts moderately
  • Remind them why you're worth it
C

Cold audience

Has never heard of you. Reached by interest or lookalike/similar targeting — the top of the funnel.

  • Interest-based or lookalike targeting
  • Largest group, lowest instant conversion
  • Introduce yourself — don't hard-sell yet
💡 Where AI fits now: tools like Meta’s Advantage+ Audience and Google’s Performance Max increasingly find warm and hot pockets for you — you feed them tracking data and a hint of who to target, and the AI expands from there. You still decide the message per temperature: cold gets an intro, warm gets a reminder, hot gets the closing nudge.
