AI Keyword & Topic Research — find the searches worth ranking for

Four hands-on activities. Sort keywords by search intent, assemble a topic-cluster research prompt block by block, run two keyword candidates through an honest validation checklist, and test your keyword-math instincts. Most content dies because nobody was searching for it — this hour is your insurance. Remember: AI suggests keywords, but the SERP decides who ranks.

Progress
1

Sort the keyword — what does the searcher actually want?

Every keyword carries an intent — the reason someone typed it. Match your content to that intent and you rank; ignore it and Google shows a different kind of page instead. Study the intent cards, then drag each keyword into its bucket. Guess freely — wrong answers come with an explanation you can hover to read.

I

Informational

The searcher wants to learn something. They're not ready to buy — they have a question.

  • Starts with how, what, why, guide, tips
  • Best served by blog posts & tutorials
  • Builds trust early in the journey
  • High volume, often lower difficulty
  • Feeds email lists and future sales
C

Commercial

The searcher is comparing options — close to buying, but weighing the choices.

  • best, review, vs, top, alternative
  • Served by comparisons & roundups
  • High buyer value, moderate volume
T

Transactional / Navigational

Ready to act now — buy, sign up, or reach a specific brand or page.

  • buy, price, coupon, near me, sign up
  • Navigational: a brand or site name
  • Highest value, lowest volume, hardest to win
