Broadcast or automated? — sort the difference

A broadcast email is a one-time send you write and blast to a list on a certain day. An automated email is set up once and then sends itself the moment a person does something — no one at a keyboard. Both matter; knowing which is which is how you stop doing by hand what should run on its own. Study the two cards, then drag each email into the bucket it truly belongs to.

B

Broadcast

A one-time send you schedule to a whole list or segment — same message, same day, to many people.

  • This week's newsletter to all subscribers
  • A flash-sale announcement on Friday
  • A product-launch email to everyone
  • You pick the send time by hand
A

Automated

Set up once, then it fires on its own when a person takes an action — you're not there when it sends.

  • A welcome email when someone signs up
  • An abandoned-cart reminder after 1 hour
  • A "happy birthday" note each year
  • Triggered by the subscriber, not you
💡 The test: did you choose the exact moment it goes out (broadcast), or does it fire on its own the instant a subscriber does something (automated)? If it sends itself while you sleep, it's automated.
