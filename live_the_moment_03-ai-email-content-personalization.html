Week 5 · AI Email Marketing

AI Email Content & Personalization — write emails people actually open

Four hands-on activities. Sort good subject lines from spammy ones, label the parts of an email that gets opened and clicked, match personalization and deliverability terms to what they mean, then quiz yourself. This is where AI stops writing generic blasts and starts writing emails that land in the inbox — and get read.

Progress
1

Sort the subject lines — what earns the open, what triggers spam

The subject line decides whether your email gets opened or ignored — and some words send you straight to the spam folder. Study the four categories, then drag each subject line into where it belongs. Wrong pills carry a hover hint explaining the right home.

💡 The core idea: AI can draft a dozen subject lines in seconds — but you decide which earns the open and which trips a spam filter. Curiosity and clarity beat hype; ALL-CAPS, "FREE!!!", and fake urgency get you filtered before anyone reads a word.

2

Fix the email draft — pick the change that gets it opened, read, and delivered

AI gives you a solid first draft — but a first draft isn't ready to send. Each email below has one problem holding it back: a spam-trigger, a fake or creepy personalization, or a design that breaks on a phone. Read each case and choose the smartest fix. A human pass like this is what separates a real email from a generic blast.

3

Label the email — match each part to what it does

Every email that gets opened and clicked has the same anatomy: from-name, subject line, preview text, body, and one clear CTA — plus the plumbing that keeps it out of spam. Knowing each part means you can brief AI precisely and spot what a draft is missing. Click a part on the left, then click what it does. A wrong match shakes red.

Personalization & deliverability — the check-yourself quiz

Five questions on subject lines, personalization that helps versus creeps, spam-avoidance, and using AI drafts responsibly. Every option explains itself — the wrong answers are the traps that turn a good email into an unopened one.

Quick reflect — make it stick

A sentence each is enough. Writing it in your own words is what moves it into memory. (Nothing is saved or sent.)

Think of the last email you sent (or would send) to your subscribers. Write a subject line and a one-line preview text for it — clear and specific, no hype. What would AI need to know to draft the body in your brand voice?
