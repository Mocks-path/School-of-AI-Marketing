An optimized page has a job in three places. The page head (the HTML Google reads first) frames what the page is about; the main content answers the searcher's intent; the supporting layer proves the page belongs and connects it to the rest of your site. Rate five content briefs for search-intent fit

Ranking starts before the first word: does the page you're about to write actually match what the searcher wants? Rate each brief on three tests — Matches the query's intent? Specific & useful (not thin)? Built on real experience / E-E-A-T? — then check yourself against the expert evaluation. The practical drill in real life: Google the target keyword, read what already ranks, and confirm your angle answers the same intent with something genuinely better before you brief the AI.
