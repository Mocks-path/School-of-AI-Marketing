Four hands-on activities. Great content that nobody sees changes nothing — this is how you get it seen and prove it worked.

Set the channel mix — slide it into the reach zone

Distribution is a balance, not a firehose. Push one piece to too few channels and almost nobody sees it; blast it to every channel at once and you spread thin, repeat yourself, and start to look like spam. Drag the slider to set how many channels you reformat this piece for. Watch effective reach, the "how it lands" read, and the live zone react. Find the sweet spot, then hit check.
