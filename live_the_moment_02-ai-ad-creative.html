2

Brand-safe or breaking policy? — sort the AI creatives

A creative that looks great in your feed can get your whole ad account paused. Ad platforms need honest claims, authentic imagery, and clear disclosure when content is AI-made or a testimonial. Study the three verdicts, then drag each real creative into the right bucket. Wrong answers carry a hover hint with the fix.

OK

Brand-safe

Ready to run as-is. It clears every ad-policy bar a platform checks.

  • Honest, provable claims only
  • Authentic, on-brand imagery
  • Testimonials disclosed and real
  • On-brand voice, no misleading urgency
FIX

Needs a fix

Good idea, one solvable flaw. A quick edit — soften the claim, add a disclosure, swap the visual — and it’s ready.

  • Overstated claim — add “results vary”
  • AI UGC with no disclosure — label it
  • Generic AI visual — make it on-brand
NO

Reject

Not worth running. A fatal flaw makes it a policy strike or a lie no matter what you tweak.

  • Flat-out false / unprovable claim
  • Fake before/after or invented review
  • Impersonates a real person or brand
💡 The feed-vs-policy gap: AI will happily write “cures anxiety in 3 days” — great hook, instant account ban. Always check claim honesty, authenticity, and disclosure before you launch. A “needs a fix” creative is often your best performer once softened and labeled; a true reject just risks your whole account.
