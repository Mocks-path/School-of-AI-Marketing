Order the journey of a post — how one video travels through the feed

When you hit "post", the app doesn't show it to everyone at once. It runs a quiet audition: a small test batch first, then wider reach only if people watch, engage, and save. Understanding this order is the whole game — it tells you what to optimise for. The five stages below are shuffled. Click them in the order a post actually moves through them — a wrong click shakes red and nudges you.

👀

Watch time

How long people actually watch before scrolling away. The single strongest signal on video feeds — a strong first three seconds decides everything.

+ Rewards a genuinely useful hook − Punishes slow, rambling intros
❤️

Engagement

Likes, comments, and replies — proof that people felt something. Comments count most, because they take real effort to leave.

+ Signals a post worth spreading − Easy to fake with bait, easy to spot
🔖

Saves & shares

When someone saves a post or sends it to a friend, they're vouching for it. The clearest sign a post earned real, lasting value.

+ Highest-quality signal of all − Hardest to earn — needs true usefulness
💡 Before you post: the feed reads all three signals — watch time, engagement, saves & shares — plus relevance to each viewer. In the activity below you'll place the five stages a post moves through in order, and see how few of the test audience it takes to unlock wide reach.

💡 Why the order matters: if a post gets shown to 500 people but stalls, the fix depends on where it stalled. Weak hook? People never watched. Watched but no comments? The idea didn't land. Knowing the stages lets you diagnose a flat post instead of just posting more and hoping. Every tactic this week plugs into one of these stages.

Match the platform to the goal it serves best

No platform is "the best" — each is built for a different job. Trying to win everywhere is how beginners burn out. Pick one or two that fit your goal. Drag each platform-and-post idea into the goal it serves best. Guess freely; wrong pills carry a hover hint with the answer.

Reach & Discovery
Getting found by brand-new audiences
Community & Trust
Building a relationship over time
Action & Conversion
Driving one clear next step
💡 The honest truth about platforms: being active on six platforms badly beats being consistent on one well — every single time. Consistency and authenticity on one or two platforms compound; spreading thin just spreads you thin. Pick where your audience already is, then show up.

💡 The lever most beginners miss: chasing more reach is like chasing a viral hit — even if it works once, it doesn't repeat.
