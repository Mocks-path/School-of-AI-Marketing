Four hands-on activities. Kill or scale — where does your budget go?

You’re running four campaigns and one budget. The smart move isn’t "spend more everywhere" — it’s to scale proven winners, give promising tests more time, tweak the fixable, and kill the losers. Drag each campaign into its priority slot, from 1 (scale it now) to 4 (kill it). Get the ranking right to finish. (All figures illustrative.)

💡 The kill-or-scale rule: put budget behind campaigns that hit or beat your break-even ROAS, give under-tested ones enough spend to judge fairly, tweak the one metric that’s broken, and kill campaigns that stay above your target CPA after a real test. Scaling a loser just loses money faster — break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ profit margin (illustrative).
2

Diagnose the campaign — creative, audience, or offer?

When a campaign underperforms, the metric that’s broken tells you where the problem is. Low CTR usually means a weak creative; the wrong people seeing it points to audience/targeting; clicks that never convert usually mean a weak offer or landing page. Drag each symptom into the layer you’d fix first. Wrong answers carry a hover hint. (Figures illustrative.)

Creative problem
Low CTR · the ad itself isn’t stopping the scroll
Audience / targeting problem
Wrong people · high CPM or irrelevant clicks
Offer / landing problem
Clicks come, but they don’t convert
💡 Read the funnel top to bottom: low CTR → fix the creative (hook, hero image, first 3 seconds); good CTR but high CPM or junk clicks → fix the audience/targeting; strong clicks that never convert → fix the offer or landing page. Change one thing at a time so you know what moved the number.
3

Read the dashboard — which campaign deserves more budget?

Here’s last week’s data for four ad campaigns. The flashy one with the most clicks isn’t always the winner — what matters is what each conversion costs (CPA) and how much revenue each ad dollar returns (ROAS). Study the table, then click the campaign you’d put more budget into. (All figures illustrative.)

Campaign Clicks Conversions CPA ROAS
How to read it: ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend and CPA = spend ÷ conversions. A campaign with fewer clicks but a low CPA and a ROAS above your break-even is the real winner — more budget multiplies a profitable campaign, but can’t rescue one that loses money on every conversion. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ your profit margin (illustrative).
