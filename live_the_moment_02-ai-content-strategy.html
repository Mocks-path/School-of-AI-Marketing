AI Content Strategy & Ideation — plan before you publish

Four hands-on activities. Build a content brief the way a strategist would, sort real workflows into "smart strategy" or "no strategy," match content ideas to the funnel stage they belong in, then pin it down with a quiz. Strategy is the layer that decides whether your content earns attention — get it right and every post works harder.

Build the content brief — one block at a time

Jumping straight to "write me a blog post" is how you get generic content nobody reads. A strategist writes a brief first, and every good brief follows a rhythm: AUDIENCE → ANGLE → SUBSTANCE → CALL TO ACTION. Who is it for and what's their pain, what promise makes them click, what real substance delivers on it, and where do they go next. The eight blocks below are scrambled. Smart strategy, or no strategy at all?

The same tools and the same hour of work produce wildly different results depending on whether a strategy sits behind them. Some habits below are how planned content compounds; others are the "just post something" trap that burns effort for nothing. Drag each workflow into the right bucket. Wrong pills hover-hint the reason.

Smart strategy
Planned, targeted, on-purpose
No strategy
Random, generic, or wasted
💡 The golden rule of content strategy: decide who it's for and what job it does before you write a word. AI can generate a hundred ideas in a minute — but without a pillar, a persona, and a purpose, you're just publishing noise faster.
