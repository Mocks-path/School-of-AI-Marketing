Sort the audience by temperature

Paid ads aren't one audience — they're three, sorted by how ready someone is to buy. A cold audience has never heard of you. A warm audience has engaged but hasn't bought. A hot audience has visited your page or cart and is ready for a nudge. Study the three temperatures, then drag each real audience into the right bucket. Guess freely — every card carries a hover hint with the answer.

CO

Cold — never met you

People who have no idea who you are. You reach them by interest and lookalike targeting. The goal is a first impression, not an instant sale.

+ Huge reach — where new customers come from + AI (Advantage+, Performance Max) finds them for you − Lowest conversion — they don’t trust you yet − Cheapest clicks, but most are just browsing
WA

Warm — engaged, no purchase

People who watched your video, followed, or liked a post but never bought. You remind them you exist and give a reason to act.

+ They already recognise your brand + Cheaper to convert than a cold stranger − Smaller audience than cold − Needs a clear offer to move them forward
HO

Hot — ready to buy

People who visited your product page or abandoned a cart. A retargeting ad and a small nudge often closes the sale.

+ Highest conversion — they already wanted it + Best return on ad spend of any audience − Smallest audience — runs out fast − Only exists if traffic came in first
💡 The core question for every audience: "How much does this person already know me?" Cold = strangers you introduce yourself to. Warm = people who engaged but didn’t buy. Hot = people who almost bought. Match the message to the temperature and you stop wasting a "buy now" ad on someone who’s never heard your name.

Follow the order — how the ad auction fires

Every time a phone opens a feed, a silent auction runs in milliseconds to decide which ad shows. Nobody bids the highest and simply wins — the platform ranks each ad by bid × relevance, then charges only what it needs to. The eight steps below are shuffled. 💡 The honest takeaway: in paid ads, the biggest cost is usually the ads themselves — nearly half the sale here. Watch what happens if the CPC rises or it takes 60 clicks instead of 40: the profit vanishes fast.
