AI Content Creation & Repurposing — drafts are the start, your voice is the moat

Four hands-on activities. Sort "AI slop" from strong, edited content; map one pillar piece into all the channel-ready pieces it can become; judge good and bad edits of an AI draft, then lock it in with a quiz. Great content isn't about generating more — it's about shaping a fast draft into something only you could publish.

Slop or strong? — sort the AI content

AI can fill a page in seconds — but half of it is generic "slop": vague, no real voice, nothing only you could say. Strong content is different: it's specific, carries a brand voice, and shows real experience. Drag each piece of content to where it belongs. Wrong pills hover-hint the reasoning.

💡 The draft is not the deliverable: AI gives you a fast first draft, never the final piece. The edit — adding a specific example, cutting the fluff, making it sound like you — is where generic text turns into content worth your brand's name.

Turn a brief into a strong draft — block by block

You brief AI to write a blog post: "How busy parents can cook healthy weeknight dinners — for MokshPath." A draft worth publishing follows a shape: HOOK → PROMISE → PROOF → CALL TO ACTION. It grabs attention, says what the reader gets, backs it with real specifics, and points them somewhere. The eight blocks are scrambled — click them in order. A wrong click explains why it doesn't fit yet.

Good edit or bad? — judge these human passes on an AI draft

The AI draft is only step one. The human editing pass — adding real experience, fact-checking, cutting fluff, sharpening the voice — is what earns trust (and E-E-A-T). But not every "edit" helps; some make it worse. Click Good or Bad on each editing move for instant feedback. Answer all six to see your score.
